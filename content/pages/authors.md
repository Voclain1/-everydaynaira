EverydayNaira is not a large newsroom. It is an independent publication, with a strong focus on clarity, honesty, and useful financial explainers for everyday Nigerians.

All content published on EverydayNaira, are written, edited, and reviewed through one clear editorial process.

Our content is educational, not personalised financial advice. That means the goal is to explain money topics clearly and responsibly, not to tell readers what financial decision to make.

Benedict Arinze Belolisah

Founder and Lead Researcher, EverydayNaira

Benedict Arinze Belolisah is the founder and lead researcher of EverydayNaira, an independent personal finance publication focused on helping Nigerians understand money in simple, practical terms.

The site was built to make everyday money topics easier to understand without jargon, pressure, or false promises. Instead of publishing content that sounds clever but leaves readers confused, EverydayNaira focuses on clear explanations, grounded examples, and practical guidance around budgeting, saving, banking, debt, salary management, and everyday financial decisions.

As editor, Benedict Arinze Belolisah researches, writes, and reviews content with a strong emphasis on clarity, source quality, and reader trust. The goal is not to sound like a financial adviser. The goal is to explain what matters, what to watch out for, and what readers need to understand before making their own decisions.

EverydayNaira publishes educational content only. It does not provide personalised financial advice, legal advice, loan recommendations, or investment advice.

> **Editorial structure**
>
> EverydayNaira is independently run. Some content may be developed with the help of research tools and internal workflows, but all published articles are reviewed before publication and held to the same editorial standards.

> **Our editorial standards**
>
> ### How EverydayNaira content is handled
>
> Topics are chosen based on practical usefulness to Nigerian readers. Articles are researched with care, written in plain English, and reviewed for clarity, accuracy, and tone before they are published.
>
> Where relevant, EverydayNaira prioritises trustworthy and verifiable sources such as the CBN, NBS, EFInA, NDIC, official institutional materials, and primary source documentation. The site does not invent statistics, fees, policies, or expertise.
>
> Every published article is educational in nature and written to help readers understand a topic more clearly, not to replace personalised financial, legal, or investment advice.
