## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website asks your browser to store on your device. They let a site remember things about your visit — for example, that you have already answered a question, or how many people visited a page.

EverydayNaira uses very few cookies. We do not use cookies to follow you around the internet, and we do not sell cookie data.

## 2. The cookies we use

### Essential cookies

These are needed for the site to work properly. They cannot be switched off, and they do not track you.

- **Your cookie choice:** when you answer the cookie banner, we store your choice on your device so we do not ask you again on every visit. This choice is stored in your browser, not on our servers.

### Analytics cookies

These help us understand how readers use the site — which guides are read, and where people stop reading — so we can write better guides. We use Google Analytics for this.

Analytics cookies only load **after you choose "Accept all"** in the cookie banner. If you choose "Essential only" or "Reject", Google Analytics does not load at all, and no analytics cookie is set.

### Advertising cookies

If adverts are shown on this site, our advertising partner Google AdSense may set cookies to show adverts. Like analytics, advertising cookies only load **after you choose "Accept all"** in the cookie banner. If you do not accept, no advertising cookie is set.

## 3. How to change your choice

You can change your cookie choice at any time:

- Click **Cookie settings** in the footer of any page. The banner will appear again and you can pick a new option.
- You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the site from working properly, but blocking analytics and advertising cookies will not break anything.

## 4. The law

This policy follows the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. Analytics and advertising cookies are not essential, so we only use them with your consent — which is why the banner asks you first, and why nothing loads until you say yes.

If you have questions about this Cookie Policy or how we handle your data, please use the form on our [Contact page](/contact/). You can also read our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/) for the full picture of what data we collect and your rights over it.
