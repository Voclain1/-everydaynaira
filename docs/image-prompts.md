# Photo prompts

Generated by `npm run images:prompts`. Paste a prompt into your image tool, generate at **16:10**, and save the best result as `image-inbox/<slug>.png` (or .jpg). Then run `npm run images:optimize`.

**Recommended settings**

- Midjourney: append `--ar 16:10 --style raw --stylize 50` and paste the avoid-list after `--no`.
- ChatGPT / GPT image, Google Gemini or Imagen, Ideogram: set the aspect ratio to 16:10 (or 3:2) and paste the prompt as is, followed by "Avoid: …".
- Flux: use raw or photo mode if your tool offers it.

**Shared style (already inside every prompt):** Editorial documentary photograph set in Nigeria, real everyday life, unposed. Shot on a full-frame camera with a 35mm lens at f/2.0, natural available light, gentle shadows, true-to-life colour with warm neutral tones and slightly muted saturation, fine natural film grain, realistic skin texture and pores, natural hands with five fingers, authentic Nigerian clothing, homes and streets. Composition with the subject in the centre third and calm space around it. No text, no readable screens, no logos or brand names, no watermarks.

**Avoid:** illustration, 3D render, CGI, cartoon, painting, plastic skin, airbrushed, HDR, oversaturated, studio backdrop, stock-photo smile, looking at camera, extra fingers, distorted hands, garbled text, logos, watermark, western suburban setting
