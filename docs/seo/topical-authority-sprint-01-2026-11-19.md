## Source and quality controls

1. Finance and banking rules must cite **original public documents** where available: CBN Guides to Charges, the CBN complaints page and NIBSS explanation of transaction disputes. The CBN-hosted 2018 instant-transfer text found in research is watermarked **DRAFT**; do not represent it alone as binding final law.
2. Treat the April 2026 CBN Guide to Charges as an **exposure draft** unless a final, clearly effective instrument is found.
3. All naira budgets are **hypothetical illustrations** with totals checked; do not claim they are national cost-of-living data.
4. The loan comparison explicitly states six equal instalments at a nominal 3% monthly rate, no fees and no missed payments; reducing-balance calculations use proper amortisation, not simple interest on shrinking annual averages.
5. Posts contain focused titles, original descriptions, key takeaways, official/external sources, contextual internal links and no fabricated “independently fact-checked” date.
6. Recheck sensitive claims before merge and when regulators publish new circulars; update source links and visible revision dates only when substantive work is done.
7. **Do not publish keyword stuffing, fake credentials, unsupported financial recommendations or a copy of competitor text**. Expert review is still advisable for legal/regulatory interpretations.

## Measurement after deployment

- Days 0–14: verify 8 canonical 200 pages, sitemap/RSS/search index and source links; inspect Google Search Console indexing and crawl.
- Every week: record impressions, clicks, CTR and average position for non-branded queries from connected GSC; filter by article/cluster.
- Month 1–3: measure first impressions, query coverage, pages receiving real clicks, internal-link referral journeys and verified editorial backlinks.
- A backlink is counted only when the linking source URL and target URL can be opened and the link is actually present. Do not count accepted pitches or promises as earned links.
- Keep 90-day goals as **targets**, not guarantees: 45–55 articles total, 8–12 referring domains and material increase in relevant non-branded impressions.
