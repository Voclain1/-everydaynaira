# EverydayNaira — editorial backlink outreach, Sprint 01

Research date: **19 November 2026**. **Status: research and draft pitches only. No messages sent, guest posts published, paid links bought or backlinks acquired by this document.**

## Editorial criteria

Seek genuine references because an article, calculation, interview or tool is useful to that publication's readers. Do not request exact-match keyword anchors, require dofollow attributes, offer link exchanges or buy placements that pass ranking credit. Respect editorial policies and any sponsorship disclosures. A publication accepting a guest contribution does **not** mean it promises a backlink.

Before outreach, confirm the page is still accepting pitches, the idea is original and not already live on EverydayNaira, and any necessary interviews or original data can be gathered truthfully.

Subject: Pitch: The five receipts Nigerians need when a transfer dispute goes nowhere

## Tracking fields for eventual outreach

For each submission: outlet, evidence URL, editor/contact route, unique pitch title, initial contact date, reply date, decision, submitted draft URL, published URL, live backlink source and target, link attribute where observable, editorial relevance and any disclosure requirement.

Avoid counting publication acceptance as an earned followed backlink. Editorial links are independent decisions.
